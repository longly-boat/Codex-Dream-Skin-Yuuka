This is an unofficial, AI-assisted Blue Archive / Yuuka fan companion. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, NEXON, NEXON Games, or the theme upstream.

The Python tool and documentation are available under the included MIT `LICENSE`.
That software license does **not** license Blue Archive characters, names, logos,
or other third-party rights. The PNG, WebP and animation previews are fan artwork,
published separately from the MIT software grant, consistent with the parent
project's artwork exclusions. Availability here is not a claim of official
authorization or a commercial artwork license.

The editable atlas comes from the existing Yuuka companion created in this
project's AI-assisted workflow. The six-frame idle row was refined with image
generation, then aligned and losslessly assembled. The other ten rows retain the
existing companion pixels. Only the retained idle-refinement prompts are included;
we do not claim to have recovered every original generation prompt or model seed.

Special thanks to the maintainers and contributors of
